# Third-party sources and redistribution boundaries

The repository licences apply only to the authors' original code, documentation, figures, and eligible aggregate results. They do not override third-party source terms.

## Materials excluded from this release

- **GDSC**: direct/near-direct response records, record-level training outputs, and frozen drug models trained from those records are excluded. The Sanger policy grants research/teaching use under source-specific conditions and does not justify blanket republication of the database or a substantial part of it. Source policy: https://depmap.sanger.ac.uk/documentation/data-usage-policy/
- **KEGG**: database files, pathway maps, and substantial pathway-membership derivative tables are excluded. Source terms: https://www.kegg.jp/kegg/legal.html
- **CellChatDB**: no database copy or full ligand–receptor export is included. CellChat software source and GPL-3.0 licence: https://github.com/sqjin/CellChat
- **cBioPortal/METABRIC/TCGA record-level derivatives**: patient/sample-level feature tables, case-flow tables, and source manifests are excluded. cBioPortal DataHub terms are source-specific and commonly use ODbL rather than the repository's code/documentation licence. See https://docs.cbioportal.org/user-guide/faq/ and https://github.com/cBioPortal/datahub
- **Public human-data records**: GEO, SCP, and I-SPY2 patient-, sample-, cell-, and spot-level exports are not redistributed here. Access them from the original repositories under their current terms.

## Materials represented only by aggregate outputs or citations

- GEO datasets GSE176078, GSE213688, GSE194040, and GSE173839 are cited and referenced by accession. NCBI encourages scientific reuse but cannot adjudicate every submitter's intellectual-property claim: https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/info/disclaimer.html
- SCP1039 is cited as the related breast cancer atlas. Terms: https://singlecell.broadinstitute.org/single_cell/terms_of_service
- DepMap/CCLE and PRISM are represented only through aggregate evaluation results and source citations in this release. Version-specific records must retain their original attribution and terms.
- GENCODE source files and symbol mappings are not redistributed here; version 44 is identified for reproducibility. Data access information: https://www.gencodegenes.org/pages/data_access.html

## Author confirmation and release boundary

On 27 October 2026, the authors confirmed ownership of the archived legacy scripts and figures, approved MIT for author-original code and CC BY 4.0 for author-original documentation, figures, and eligible aggregate tables, and confirmed that Figure 1 had not previously been published or licensed to a third party. The retained tables were screened as non-record-level aggregate or quality-assurance outputs and do not reproduce a substantial part of a restricted database.
